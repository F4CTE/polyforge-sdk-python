## 2. Service Description

PolyForge is a **software-as-a-service (SaaS) strategy automation platform** that enables users to design, backtest, and automate trading strategies for use on Polymarket, a third-party decentralized prediction market platform.

### 2.1 Core Service Features

The Service provides:

- **Strategy Builder:** A visual, block-based interface for constructing algorithmic trading strategies using configurable logic blocks, conditions, and parameters.
- **Backtesting Engine:** Historical simulation of strategies against Polymarket market data to evaluate hypothetical past performance.
- **Paper Trading:** Simulated live trading without real funds, for strategy evaluation in current market conditions.
- **Live Strategy Execution:** Automated execution of strategies on Polymarket using credentials and wallet connections provided by the User.
- **Market Data Feeds:** Access to real-time and historical Polymarket market data aggregated through the PolyForge platform.
- **Notification Services:** Alerts via email, Telegram, Discord, or webhook for strategy events and order fills.
- **Analytics and Reporting:** Performance dashboards and analytics for active and historical strategies.

### 2.2 What PolyForge Is Not

PolyForge is a **software tool only**. PolyForge:

- Is not a broker-dealer, exchange, designated contract market (DCM), swap execution facility (SEF), or money services business.
- Does not hold, custody, manage, or have access to user funds or digital assets; all assets remain in the User's own Polymarket account and connected wallet.
- Does not provide financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.
- Does not guarantee or represent the profitability of any strategy.
- Is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Polymarket or any prediction market operator.
