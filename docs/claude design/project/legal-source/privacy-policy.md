## 1. Introduction and Scope

PolyForge, Inc. ("PolyForge," "we," "us," or "our") operates the PolyForge platform — a SaaS strategy automation tool for Polymarket prediction markets. This Privacy Policy explains:

- what personal data we collect about you and why;
- how we use, store, and protect it;
- how long we keep it;
- who we share it with; and
- what rights you have over your data.

This Policy applies to all users of the PolyForge website, platform, APIs, and associated services (collectively, the "Service"), regardless of where you are located. Where applicable, it supplements (and does not replace) our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md) and [Cookie Policy](./cookie-policy.md).

**Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland** benefit from additional rights and protections under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable national laws. We describe those specifically in Sections 6, 14, and 17.

**California residents** have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). Those rights are described in Section 15.

## 4. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

### 4.1 Account and Identity Data

| Data Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Email address | Required for account creation, login, and communications |
| Username / display name | User-chosen identifier within the platform |
| Password hash | Bcrypt hash stored securely — plaintext is never retained |
| Account creation date | Timestamp of registration |
| Account status | Active, suspended, or deleted flag |

### 4.2 Usage and Behavioural Data

| Data Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Log data (server-side) | IP address, request timestamps, HTTP method/path, response codes, user-agent string |
| Session data | Session tokens stored in encrypted cookies; session start/end times |
| Feature usage events | Which platform features you use (strategy builder, backtesting, notifications), without recording the content of your strategies |
| Error and crash reports | Stack traces and error codes to diagnose platform issues |

### 4.3 Trading Strategy Data

| Data Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Strategy configurations | Block definitions, conditions, parameters, and wiring you create in the strategy builder |
| Backtest inputs and results | Historical simulation parameters and aggregated performance output |
| Paper trade history | Simulated order records for strategies run in paper-trading mode |

Strategy data is treated as your intellectual property. See Section 10 of the [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md) for ownership terms.

### 4.4 Polymarket Wallet and API Credentials

| Data Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Polymarket API credentials | Encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM in our Rust WASM signer module; private keys never pass through the V8 runtime |
| Wallet address (public) | Public-key identifier used to associate orders with your Polymarket account |
| EIP-712 signed payloads | Signatures generated on your behalf when submitting orders |

We do **not** store private keys in plaintext under any circumstances. The signer service is architecturally isolated and operates exclusively within an encrypted Rust/WASM context.

| Billing records | Invoice history, plan change dates, payment status |
| Payment method metadata | Last 4 digits, card brand, and expiry — provided by Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers |

Full payment card data is handled exclusively by **Stripe, Inc.** under their own PCI-DSS certified infrastructure. See Section 8.

### 4.6 Communications Data

| Data Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Support messages | Content of support requests you submit |
| Email correspondence | Emails exchanged with our team |
| Notification preferences | Your chosen notification channels and alert configurations |
| Feedback and survey responses | Optional responses to product surveys |

### 4.7 Technical and Device Data

| Data Element | Description |
|---|---|

## 6. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

For EEA, UK, and Swiss users, every processing activity must have a lawful basis under Art. 6 GDPR. The table below maps our processing activities to their legal basis.

| Processing Activity | Legal Basis | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Account creation and management | **Contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) | Necessary to provide the Service you signed up for |
| Authentication and session management | **Contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) | Necessary to maintain your logged-in state |
| Strategy creation, storage, and execution | **Contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) | Core feature delivery |
| Order submission to Polymarket | **Contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) | Necessary to carry out instructions you explicitly initiate |
| Billing and payment processing | **Contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) | Necessary to process your subscription |
| Security logging and fraud prevention | **Legitimate Interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) | Our interest in protecting the platform and all users from abuse; does not override your rights |
| Platform usage analytics | **Legitimate Interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) | Improving product quality and user experience; you may opt out at any time |
| Marketing communications (email) | **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) | Only sent where you have opted in; you may withdraw consent at any time |
| Compliance with legal obligations | **Legal Obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c)) | Tax records, regulatory requests, court orders |
| Dispute resolution and legal claims | **Legitimate Interests** / **Legal Claims** (Art. 6(1)(f) / Art. 9(2)(f)) | Defending or pursuing legal claims |

Where we rely on **Legitimate Interests**, we have conducted a balancing test and determined that our interests do not override your fundamental rights and freedoms. You may request our Legitimate Interests Assessment by contacting [dpo@polyforge.app](mailto:dpo@polyforge.app).

## 10. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law.

| Data Category | Retention Period | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Account data (email, username) | Duration of account + 30 days after deletion | Allows account recovery within grace period |
| Strategy configurations and orders | Duration of account + 90 days after deletion | Allows export; then permanently deleted |
| Server and access logs | 90 days | Security monitoring and incident response |
| Billing records and invoices | 7 years | Tax and accounting obligations |
| Support correspondence | 3 years from resolution | Dispute resolution |
| Marketing consent records | Until consent withdrawn + 3 years | Compliance evidence |
| Encrypted API credentials | Deleted immediately upon user deletion or credential removal | No retention of signing keys |

After the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or irreversibly anonymised. Anonymised aggregate analytics data (with no link to any individual) may be retained indefinitely.

**Account deletion:** You may delete your account at any time via Settings → Account → Delete Account. Within 30 days of deletion, we will permanently erase all personal data except where retention is legally required (e.g. billing records).

## 12. Security Measures

We implement technical and organisational measures designed to protect your personal data:

**Technical measures:**
- All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS 1.3
- API credentials encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM within an isolated Rust/WASM signer module
- Password hashing using bcrypt (cost factor ≥ 12)
- Session tokens stored in HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict cookies
- Database encrypted at rest
- Role-based access controls limiting employee access to personal data
- Regular dependency and vulnerability scanning

**Organisational measures:**
- Access to production systems limited to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis
- Security training for all team members
- Incident response plan with 72-hour breach notification obligation (GDPR Art. 33)
- Annual security audits

**Data breach notification:** If we experience a breach likely to result in high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you without undue delay (and within 72 hours for supervisory authority notification under GDPR).

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we commit to treating any incident with urgency and transparency.

## 14. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the following rights under GDPR (and the UK GDPR / Swiss nFADP respectively):

### 14.1 Right of Access (Art. 15)

You have the right to obtain confirmation of whether we process personal data about you and, if so, a copy of that data along with information about how it is processed.

### 14.2 Right to Rectification (Art. 16)

You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal data or completion of incomplete data.

### 14.3 Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17)

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data where:
- the data is no longer necessary for the original purpose;
- you withdraw consent (where consent was the legal basis);
- you object to processing based on legitimate interests and we have no overriding grounds;
- the data has been unlawfully processed; or
- deletion is required by law.

This right does not apply where retention is required by law (e.g. billing records) or for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

### 14.4 Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18)

You have the right to request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances, including while you contest its accuracy or object to processing.

### 14.5 Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)

Where processing is based on consent or contract and is carried out by automated means, you have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (JSON or CSV) and to transmit it to another controller.

This right applies to: your account data, your strategy configurations, and your order history.

### 14.6 Right to Object (Art. 21)

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)), including for profiling or direct marketing purposes. We will cease that processing unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing is necessary for legal claims.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link in any marketing email or via Settings → Notifications.

### 14.7 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making (Art. 22)

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects. If this changes, we will update this Policy and obtain the necessary safeguards.

### 14.8 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where we process your data based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

## 15. Your Rights Under CCPA (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), grants you the following rights:

### 15.1 Categories of Personal Information Collected

We collect the following categories as defined by the CCPA:

| CCPA Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Identifiers | Email, username, IP address |
| Personal information (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.80(e)) | Name, email |
| Commercial information | Subscription plan, billing records |
| Internet or network activity | Browsing history on the platform, feature usage |
| Geolocation data | IP-inferred approximate location |
| Inferences | Usage preferences derived from platform behaviour |

### 15.2 Sources of Personal Information

We collect personal information from: (a) you directly; (b) automatically via your use of the Service; and (c) third-party services (Stripe, Polymarket API).

### 15.3 Business or Commercial Purposes for Collection

We collect personal information to: provide and improve the Service, process payments, ensure security, comply with legal obligations, and communicate with you. See Section 7 for full detail.

### 15.4 Categories Shared with Third Parties

We share identifiers and commercial information with Stripe (payment processing) and infrastructure providers. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

### 15.5 Your CCPA Rights

**Right to Know:** You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you over the past 12 months, the sources, the business purposes, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.

**Right to Delete:** You may request that we delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions (e.g. legal obligations, completing transactions).

**Right to Correct:** You may request correction of inaccurate personal information.

**Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing:** We do **not** sell your personal information for money. We do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. As a result, there is currently no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" action to take. If this changes, we will update this Policy and provide a prominent opt-out link.

**Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information:** We do not use sensitive personal information (as defined by the CPRA) beyond what is necessary to perform the Service. We do not use it for inferences about you.

**Right to Non-Discrimination:** We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights. We will not deny goods or services, charge different prices, or provide a different quality of service because you exercised your privacy rights.

### 15.6 How California Residents Submit Requests

See Section 16. We respond to verifiable consumer requests within **45 days**, with one possible 45-day extension for complex requests (with notice).
