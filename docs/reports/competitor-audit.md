# Polyforge — Competitor Feature Audit
**199 Polymarket Builders Program Participants**
*Ranked by cumulative volume routed to Polymarket — March 2026*

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This document audits all 199 projects registered in the Polymarket Builders Program leaderboard, documenting the features each offers and how they position relative to Polyforge.

**Key observations:**

- The top 10 platforms account for the vast majority of volume (>$330M combined), led by Betmoar ($195.77M) — more than all competitors combined for most of 2024.
- The competitive landscape clusters into 6 primary archetypes: Professional Web Terminals, Mobile Apps, Telegram Bots (copy trading / execution), AI Analytics Platforms, Whale Tracking / Alerts, and Aggregators / Arbitrage tools.
- Copy trading is the most contested feature space (PolyCop, Polygun, Stand.trade, Olympus, Polycule, Ratio, CarbonCopy, Polydupe, PolyCopyBot, copytrade.gg, and many more).
- The long tail (ranks 121–199) contains mostly micro-projects with <$10K cumulative volume, many of which are early-stage or inactive.
- Very few platforms combine trading terminal + analytics + alerts + social + fund management in a single product — this remains an open competitive opportunity.

## 6. Competitive Gaps & Opportunities for Polyforge

### → Copy Trading with Risk Controls + Self-Custody
Most copy bots are either fast (PolyCop) or feature-rich but custodial (Polycule). A non-custodial bot with granular risk controls (stop-loss, max exposure, win-rate filters) is underserved.

### → Unified Terminal + Telegram Bot in One Product
Betmoar dominates the web terminal space but has no native mobile or Telegram trading. Polygun does Telegram but no web analytics. No single platform combines both well.

### → Portfolio-Level Automation (Take-Profit / Stop-Loss across positions)
Stand.trade offers this but it's complex. A simpler, mobile-accessible TP/SL system with notification-based confirmations would attract mid-tier traders.

### → Fund Management with Transparency
PolyFund addresses this at $194K volume. It remains a small niche. There is significant opportunity in a properly governed, transparent on-chain fund product.

### → Cross-Platform Arbitrage with Auto-Execution
Scanners exist (NoArb, oddsjuicer) but auto-execution across Polymarket + Kalshi is rare and technically complex. A verified, audited arb executor would be highly valuable.

### → News-to-Trade Pipeline
Betmoar has news integration but does not auto-suggest trades from news events. An LLM-powered pipeline that reads breaking news, identifies related markets, and surfaces a recommended trade has no dominant player.

### → Social Reputation / Prediction History
No platform has built a persistent on-chain trader reputation system (e.g. decentralised track record NFT / scoring). This is an open infrastructure gap.

### → Educational Onboarding for Newcomers
Most tools assume expertise. A platform combining beginner education with paper-trading/simulation mode and then live trading is missing.
