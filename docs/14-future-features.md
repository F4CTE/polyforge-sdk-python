# Polyforge — Future Features

> These features are documented for planning purposes. **None are currently being implemented.**
> They represent potential directions based on competitive analysis, user feedback, and platform evolution.

---

## Strategic Framing

Polyforge is a **layer over Polymarket** — a Bloomberg terminal + algo execution engine for prediction markets. It enhances what users can do with their own Polymarket account. **Polyforge does not custody or manage user funds.** Every trade is executed in the user's own Polymarket account via their API key.

This constraint shapes every feature: Polyforge improves **information**, **execution**, and **automation** — never holding capital or acting as a fund manager.

## Smart Order Execution ✅ *Shipped v6.12.0*

Replace single-click orders with institutional-grade execution, all transparently managed within the user's own account.

### Scope

- **TWAP (Time-Weighted Average Price)** — enter or exit a position in equal tranches over a user-defined time window; minimizes market impact on large positions
- **Dollar-Cost Averaging** — recurring buy orders on a schedule (e.g. buy $50 of YES every Monday until resolution); configurable amount, interval, and end condition
- **Scale-in / Scale-out** — split a position into N tranches triggered by price levels rather than time; e.g. buy 20% at 0.40, 30% at 0.35, 50% at 0.30
- **Iceberg Orders** — show only a fraction of the order on the book; auto-refill from hidden reserve as fills occur; prevents frontrunning of large positions
- **Bracket Orders** — single-click bundle: entry order + take-profit order + stop-loss order placed simultaneously; any fill automatically links the TP/SL to the actual filled quantity
- **One-Cancels-Other (OCO)** — two orders linked so that when one fills, the other is automatically cancelled

### Requirements

- New strategy block type: `EXECUTION_ALGORITHM` wrapping the existing order pipeline
- Scheduler in strategy-engine for time-based tranching
- Order linkage model in the Order schema (parentOrderId, linkedOrders)

## Portfolio Risk Intelligence

Analytics and guardrails that make every user trade more safely — using their own data, no custody required.

### Scope

- **Kelly Criterion Position Sizer** — user inputs their confidence level; Polyforge calculates optimal position size based on current portfolio balance and Kelly formula; displayed as a suggestion before order placement
- **Portfolio-Level Drawdown Circuit Breaker** ✅ *Shipped v6.10.0* — single global setting: if total portfolio value drops X% in Y hours, automatically pause all running strategies; configurable lookback (1h–7d), threshold slider (1–50%), Settings → Risk tab + Portfolio alert banner
- **Correlation Heat Map** — visual breakdown of capital exposure by market topic/category (elections, crypto, sports, macro); flag over-concentration above a configurable threshold
- **P&L Attribution** — break down realized and unrealized P&L by market, strategy, category, and time period; answer "which bets made money and why"
- **Resolution Calendar** — markets expiring within 7/30/90 days sorted by price uncertainty (closest to 0.50); surface high-value opportunities for traders who believe the market is mispriced
- **Position-Level Risk Score** — per-position display of time-to-resolution, liquidity depth, and proximity to stop-loss; visible on the portfolio page

### Requirements

- Kelly formula calculation endpoint in api-service
- Portfolio snapshot time-series query for drawdown calculation
- Category tagging on market records for heat map grouping

## Strategy Marketplace ✅ *Shipped v6.13.0*

A two-sided marketplace where strategy creators earn revenue from users who run their strategies — network effects flywheel that no competitor has built.

### Scope

- **Strategy publishing** — creator sets a strategy as "For Sale" or "For Rent"; configures price (one-time or monthly subscription)
- **Strategy renting** — buyer pays and receives a copy that runs in their own account; strategy logic is visible (open marketplace, not black-box)
- **Performance fee model** — optional: creator earns a % of profits generated by the rented strategy (configurable 0–20%); calculated weekly, paid in USDC from the renter's account
- **Verified performance metrics** — strategy performance is computed from on-chain trade history, not self-reported; displayed as backtested P&L + live P&L since publication
- **Discovery and search** — filter by category, risk level, time horizon, minimum capital, verified win rate
- **Strategy reviews** — renters leave ratings and written reviews after 30+ days of usage
- **Creator dashboard** — revenue earned, active subscribers, strategy performance breakdown
- **Strategy challenges** — time-boxed competitions ("Best-performing crypto strategy this month wins $500"); drives user-generated content and press coverage

### Requirements

- Marketplace pricing model in the Strategy schema
- Performance fee calculation job (weekly cron, triggered by strategy P&L snapshot)
- Creator payout flow (USDC transfer or credit to Polyforge balance)
- Discovery feed API with sorting by verified live performance

## Prediction Accuracy & Calibration ✅ *Shipped v6.15.0*

Track not just P&L but the quality of a trader's probability estimates — a unique metric that prediction markets make possible.

### Scope

- **Calibration score** — measure whether a user's predictions match reality: when they said 70% probability, did the event happen ~70% of the time? Displayed as a calibration curve
- **Brier score** — industry-standard scoring rule for probabilistic predictions; lower is better
- **Accuracy by category** — breakdown of calibration per market category; reveals whether a user is better at politics vs. crypto vs. sports
- **Public accuracy badge** — shareable profile badge displaying verified calibration score; opt-in display on public profile
- **Accuracy leaderboard** — rank users by calibration quality, not just P&L; rewards skilled forecasters, not lucky gamblers
- **Historical accuracy timeline** — track improvement over time; motivates skill development

### Requirements

- Calibration score calculation job consuming resolved market history and user's order history
- Brier score per resolved position stored in the analytics schema
- Public profile accuracy section (opt-in)

## Merge Arbitrage (Built on Split/Merge Primitives) ✅ *Shipped v6.11.0*

A fully automated strategy that exploits a fundamental pricing inefficiency in prediction markets — with zero directional risk.

### Background

In Polymarket's conditional token framework, YES + NO tokens on the same market always settle to a combined $1.00 at resolution. If the market price of YES + NO < $1.00 (e.g., YES at $0.47 + NO at $0.51 = $0.98), buying both sides and merging them back into USDC yields a risk-free $0.02 profit per dollar deployed.

### Scope

- **Merge arbitrage scanner** — continuously monitor all active markets for YES + NO combined price below a configurable threshold (e.g., < $0.99 after fees)
- **One-click arb execution** — buy both sides simultaneously at current prices, then merge to USDC; displayed as a single "Execute" button with expected profit shown
- **Automated arb strategy** — strategy block that auto-executes merge arbitrage when detected above a profit threshold; runs in the user's own account
- **Arb opportunity feed** — real-time list of current merge arb opportunities across all markets, sorted by profit per dollar deployed
- **Split-based liquidity provision** — complement to arb: when YES + NO > $1.00 (rare), split USDC and sell both sides for risk-free profit

### Requirements

- Real-time price sum calculation across all active markets (market-data-service)
- Split/merge order primitives exposed as strategy engine action blocks
- Arb scanner service with configurable profit threshold

### User-Facing Framing

Users see "Earn risk-free yield" and "Market Making" — not "split/merge." The underlying mechanics are invisible.

## AI Portfolio Optimizer ✅ *Shipped v6.15.0*

Personalized, actionable suggestions powered by the user's own trading history — not generic advice.

### Scope

- **Weekly portfolio review** — auto-generated summary of what happened to the portfolio, which strategies performed, which markets resolved and how
- **Rebalancing suggestions** — "You have 65% exposure in US politics with elections 30 days away — consider reducing" with one-click strategy adjustments
- **Confidence-based position sizer** — user types "I'm 80% confident this resolves YES" → AI calculates suggested position size using Kelly formula + current portfolio state
- **Hedge suggestions** — detect when a user is heavily concentrated in one outcome and surface correlated markets that could hedge the exposure
- **"What if" simulator** — replay historical market outcomes against a proposed strategy to estimate expected performance
- **Auto trade journal** — structured weekly report: P&L summary, best/worst trades, execution quality score, actionable improvements

### Requirements

- LLM integration in api-service (Claude API, GPT-4o fallback — already exists)
- User trade history analysis pipeline
- Portfolio composition snapshot for weekly context

## Sentiment Intelligence ✅ *Shipped v6.15.0*

Real-time signal layer connecting news and social media to prediction market prices.

### Scope

- **Sentiment overlay on market cards** — small indicator showing whether social/news sentiment for the market's underlying topic has shifted in the last 4 hours: bullish / bearish / neutral
- **Sentiment feed** — curated news and social signals tied to a user's open positions and watchlist markets; surfaces relevant information without information overload
- **Sentiment-triggered strategy blocks** — new block type: `SENTIMENT_SIGNAL` that fires when confidence and direction threshold is met; chains into existing strategy logic
- **Source diversity** — aggregate from: Twitter/X accounts tagged as prediction market commentators, major news RSS feeds, Reddit political/crypto communities, official government sources for political markets
- **Sentiment history** — chart overlay showing sentiment score over time alongside market price; identify sentiment-price divergences as trading signals

### Requirements

- News/social ingestion pipeline (already partially built — ai-service)
- Per-market sentiment score computed on each ingestion cycle
- Sentiment signal WebSocket events for real-time overlay updates

## Tax Reporting Assistant

Help users understand their own trade history for tax purposes — display only, no advice, no custody.

### Scope

- **Cost basis tracking** — compute cost basis per position using FIFO, LIFO, or average cost methods; toggle between methods
- **Realized P&L export** — CSV export of all closed positions with entry price, exit price, size, realized gain/loss, and timestamps; formatted for common tax software (TurboTax, Koinly, etc.)
- **Tax year summary** — total realized gains, total realized losses, net position for a given tax year
- **Lot-level detail** — show which specific buy lots were consumed by each sale (relevant for tax loss harvesting)
- **Disclaimer** — clearly marked as informational only; Polyforge is not a tax advisor

### Requirements

- Cost basis calculation from order history (FIFO/LIFO/average cost)
- Export endpoint returning structured CSV
- Tax year filter in the portfolio analytics API

## Arbitrage Scanner

Cross-platform price comparison engine that identifies arbitrage opportunities between prediction market platforms.

### Scope

- **Cross-platform price comparison** — real-time price feeds from Polymarket and Kalshi displayed side-by-side for equivalent markets
- **Arbitrage opportunity detection** — automated scanning for price discrepancies that exceed a configurable threshold (e.g., 2%+ spread)
- **Auto-execution** — optional automated placement of offsetting positions across platforms when profitable arbitrage is detected; each platform executes in the user's own account on that platform
- **Risk dashboard** — track open arbitrage positions, net exposure, and P&L across platforms
- **Settlement risk monitoring** — alert when correlated markets have different resolution timelines or criteria

### Requirements

- Kalshi API integration (REST + WebSocket)
- Market mapping layer to match equivalent markets across platforms
- Sub-second execution latency for competitive arbitrage
- Position reconciliation across platforms

### Scope

## LP / Market Making ✅ *Shipped v6.15.0*

Automated liquidity provision and spread management tools — built transparently on top of the split/merge primitives. Users see "Provide Liquidity," not "split tokens."

### Scope

- **"Provide Liquidity" button on market detail page** — user sets capital amount and target spread; Polyforge splits USDC into YES + NO tokens and places two-sided quotes automatically
- **Spread management** — configurable target spread, minimum spread, and spread adjustment based on volatility and time-to-resolution
- **Inventory management** — auto-rebalancing to maintain neutral inventory with configurable drift tolerance; merges imbalanced inventory back to USDC
- **LP performance dashboard** — P&L attribution separating spread income from directional inventory risk; fill rate, market share, and capital utilization
- **Multi-market market making** — run liquidity provision across multiple markets simultaneously with shared capital allocation
- **Spread optimizer** — analyze order flow velocity and time-to-resolution to suggest optimal spread per market

### User-Facing Framing

"Earn spread income as a market maker" — split/merge mechanics are invisible implementation details.

### Requirements

- High-frequency order management (sub-second quote updates)
- Real-time order book depth analysis
- Position and inventory tracking at the token level
- Split/merge primitives as internal strategy engine calls

## UMA Oracle Dashboard

Monitoring and analytics for UMA oracle disputes that affect Polymarket market resolutions.

### Scope

- **Track disputed Polymarket resolutions** — real-time monitoring of markets entering the UMA dispute process
- **Vote monitoring** — display UMA governance votes related to Polymarket market resolutions
- **Early warning system** — alert users holding positions in markets with active or pending disputes
- **Dispute history** — historical record of past disputes, outcomes, and resolution timelines
- **Position impact analysis** — calculate potential P&L impact of dispute outcomes on user portfolios

### Requirements

- UMA protocol API or on-chain event monitoring
- Polymarket resolution event correlation
- Historical dispute data ingestion
- Real-time WebSocket events for dispute state changes
